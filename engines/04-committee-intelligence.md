# Engine 04 — Committee & Organisation Intelligence

## Copilot-ready instruction
> Reconstruct this body's governance identity from authoritative public or approved organisational sources. Determine its current name and status, purpose, legal or constitutional basis, decision/advisory/scrutiny role, membership rules, chair, quorum where evidenced, terms of reference, delegated/reserved functions, reporting and scrutiny relationships, meeting cycle, publication/public-participation rules, recurring business, key professional interfaces, predecessors/successors and significant dependencies. Label each material field DOCUMENTED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN and cite its source. Prefer current constitutional/enabling material over historical minutes. Never infer powers from the body name. Produce the standard Body Profile plus unresolved questions.

## Discovery preference
Enabling instrument/current constitution → terms of reference → official body page → calendar → agendas/reports → decisions/minutes → forward plan → membership → history.
