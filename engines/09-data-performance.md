# Engine 09 — Data & Performance

## Copilot-ready instruction
> Analyse the supplied operational data to understand process performance, quality and variation. Check definitions, denominator, time period and missingness before calculation. Distinguish counts from rates and workload from quality. Identify uncertainty and data-quality limitations. Look for bottlenecks, rework, aging work, repeated defects and changes over time. Do not infer individual competence from weak process proxies. Return findings, limitations, questions and the smallest useful next measurement.
