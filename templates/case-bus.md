### Handoff rules
- Never silently promote an inference into VERIFIED FACTS.
- Never discard a contradiction or unknown during summarisation.
- Never invent authority, permission, deadline or tool capability.
- Do not put confidential workplace information or unnecessary personal data into a public or unapproved system.
- Use the smallest engine combination needed.
- Stop when the bounded outcome is complete.
