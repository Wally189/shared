## New mail

1. Open the mailbox.
2. Read the new message.
3. Decide: ACTION / WAITING / REFERENCE / RECORD / ROUTINE / SENSITIVE-REVIEW.
4. Follow the relevant step below.

## ACTION

State exactly where it moves/is flagged, who owns it and how the deadline is recorded.

## WAITING

State how follow-up is recorded and when it is reviewed.

## REFERENCE / RECORD

State when email may remain reference and when it must be saved/recorded in another approved system.

## ROUTINE / TRANSITORY

State the locally approved retention/deletion rule. Do not invent one.

## SENSITIVE / REVIEW

Do not add commentary or copy content into another uncontrolled location.
Follow the organisation's Information Governance / Records / HR / Legal route as appropriate.

## What not to do

- do not create subjective personal notes about people in the mailbox;
- do not use colour alone to encode critical status;
- do not bulk-delete because material looks old;
- do not change retention/archive settings without authority;
- do not create PST/local archives unless organisational policy explicitly permits it;
- do not forward sensitive mail to personal/private storage;
- do not make the mailbox the only copy of a record that belongs elsewhere.
