Decision integrity, not meeting administration

The operating model

The useful unit is not “a meeting”. It is a decision journey: authority → evidence → route → consideration → decision → record → action → memory.

Three truth layers

National baseline

Legislation, secondary legislation, statutory guidance, information law, accessibility requirements and general professional standards.

Local governance

The body's enabling instruments, constitution, standing orders, terms of reference, schemes of delegation and current local procedures.

Live case

The particular paper, meeting, decision, action or governance question.

Never skip the middle layer. “The legislation generally provides…” does not become “therefore this committee has power to…” until authority type, commencement, local instruments and delegation have been checked.

Professional boundaries

Democratic Services should know how to work with Legal/Monitoring Officer, Finance/S151, information governance/DPO, audit, communications, digital/IT, procurement, elections and policy/programme teams without silently absorbing their authority. Knowing when another professional must answer is itself a governance capability.

A sixth concern: adoption

The five-layer architecture describes the operating system. Moving an existing service towards it requires a separate adoption discipline. A functioning legacy process should not be replaced merely because a newer tool exists. Observe the actual work, preserve tacit knowledge, remove ambiguity, prove a smaller method, support users in context, then migrate only when the replacement is demonstrably easier or more reliable.
