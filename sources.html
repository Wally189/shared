Current authority, applicability and provenance

Source register

A source register is more useful than a bibliography because it records what proposition a source controls, to whom it applies, and whether it is current.

Source discipline

  • Verify jurisdiction and authority type.
  • Record published and effective dates separately.
  • Check commencement and savings/transitional provisions.
  • Distinguish law from guidance and practice.
  • Prefer current primary authority for propositions it controls.
  • Keep superseded sources as history.
  • Do not turn AI summaries into authority.

Tool diagnosis, writing and professional communication sources

  • Microsoft product guidance About Power Query in Excel — connect, transform, combine and load external/current-workbook data.
  • Microsoft product guidance Power Query data sources in Excel versions — source and refresh support differs across Excel environments.
  • Microsoft product guidance Manage workbook links — workbook links are a separate feature with their own refresh/trust behaviour.
  • Microsoft product guidance Manage external data connections — queries/connections and refresh behaviour.
  • Government writing standard Functional Standards writing style guide — plain English, short sentences, one idea at a time and clear mandatory/advisory distinctions.
  • Government service guidance Writing emails and messages — put important information first, give clear instructions, deadlines and consequences.
  • Transferable communication standard GovS 011 Communication — clear, concise, consistent, factual and accountable communication; local government may use it for benchmarking.
  • Government behaviour-change practice Principles of behaviour change — understand audience barriers, capability, opportunity/support and motivation; use proportionately and ethically.

Mailbox, Outlook and information-governance sources

  • Microsoft product guidance Best practices for Outlook — simple folders, rules/Quick Steps, categories and organisational retention-policy caveats.
  • Microsoft product guidance Manage mailbox size — classic Outlook Mailbox Cleanup can inspect folder size and find old/large items.
  • Microsoft product guidance Archive in Outlook — Archive does not itself reduce Microsoft 365/Exchange mailbox size; Online Archive is separate where available.
  • Microsoft product guidance Shared mailboxes — intended for groups to monitor/send from a common address; admin permissions apply.
  • Microsoft product guidance Shared mailbox settings in new Outlook — supported settings and current client/permission limitations.
  • Microsoft records feature Retention policies in Outlook — organisations can apply retention policies to preserve or remove mail according to policy/legal need.
  • ICO data-protection guidance Data minimisation — personal data must be adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary.
  • ICO records guidance Retention — document retention periods and actions after retention, aligned to business/legal need.

Quiet-modernisation sources

  • Government service practice How to apply the Service Standard — complex legacy environments cannot be solved in one go; start small enough to learn and iterate.
  • Government legacy guidance Moving away from legacy systems — understand the existing landscape and user needs before change, and involve affected stakeholders.
  • Government service practice Iterate and improve frequently — services remain relevant by making improvements throughout their lifetime.
  • Government adoption practice Encouraging people to use a digital service — understand why people do not adopt the digital route and provide assisted support rather than simply forcing channel shift.
  • Government data-quality guidance Implementing a data quality action plan — identify critical data, define quality rules, baseline current quality, investigate root causes and prioritise improvements.
  • Microsoft product guidance Excel data validation — restrict inputs to appropriate values/types and provide user guidance/error messages.
  • Microsoft product guidance Lists column types and column validation — Choice/Lookup and validation can keep structured data consistent.
  • Microsoft product guidance Versioning in Lists and libraries — retain, inspect and restore earlier versions instead of encoding every revision into a new filename.
  • Microsoft adoption guidance Champion Programme best practices — peer champions support adoption and encouragement.
  • Microsoft training guidance Streamline user training — put training in the context of day-to-day tasks and accommodate different learning styles.
  • Public-sector improvement practice NHS England change model — shared purpose, ownership, motivation, measurement, improvement tools and spread/adoption.
  • Accessibility standard WCAG 2.2: Use of Color — colour must not be the sole visual means of conveying information.

Local-organisation discovery source hierarchy

For local discovery, prefer the organisation's current official sources in this order: governance/democracy landing page; constitution or governing documents; enabling instruments; current body/committee directory; calendars/agendas/minutes; forward plan or equivalent; officer/delegated decisions; published decision records; transparency/accounts/assurance material. The organisation itself is deliberately not named on this public reference site.
