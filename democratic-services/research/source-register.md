## Priority source families

Research should normally consider, as applicable:

1. legislation.gov.uk and current statutory instruments;
2. GOV.UK statutory guidance and competent government publications;
3. ICO;
4. Local Government Association;
5. Centre for Governance and Scrutiny and equivalent sector bodies;
6. CIPFA / relevant professional bodies where public material is available;
7. Electoral Commission where within scope;
8. Local Government & Social Care Ombudsman / relevant independent bodies;
9. Government Functional Standards and Civil Service professional guidance where genuinely transferable;
10. published constitutions, delegations and committee arrangements from public bodies;
11. Parliament / parliamentary committee practice;
12. NHS/public-sector board governance;
13. universities;
14. corporate/company-secretariat practice;
15. records management, accessibility, service design and digital-government standards.

## Source-control rules

- Verify jurisdiction and effective/current date.
- Record commencement where legally material.
- Distinguish law from guidance and practice.
- Record authority-type applicability.
- Prefer current primary authority for propositions it controls.
- Preserve superseded sources only as history.
- Do not treat copied snippets or AI summaries as source authority.
