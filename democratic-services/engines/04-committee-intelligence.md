# Engine 04 — Committee & Organisation Intelligence

## Purpose

Reconstruct a democratic body's current identity, authority, relationships, cycle and institutional history from authoritative material.

## Discovery preference

`enabling instrument / constitution → terms of reference → official body page → calendar → agendas/reports → decisions/minutes → forward plan → membership records → historic material`

## Output

Use `../../templates/body-profile.md`.

## Quality checks

- current/superseded state identified;
- decision/advisory/scrutiny status evidenced;
- terms of reference/power source located;
- parent/reporting relationships source-linked;
- historical practice not mistaken for current authority;
- predecessor/successor links explicit.

## Prohibited behaviour

- inferring powers from a body's name;
- treating an old minute as proof of current delegation;
- fabricating quorum, chair or membership rules.

## Copilot-ready instruction

> Reconstruct this body's governance identity from authoritative public or approved organisational sources. Determine its current name/status, purpose, legal or constitutional basis, decision/advisory/scrutiny role, membership rules, chair, quorum where evidenced, terms of reference, delegated/reserved functions, reporting and scrutiny relationships, meeting cycle, publication/public-participation rules, recurring business, key professional interfaces, predecessors/successors and significant dependencies. Label each material field DOCUMENTED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN and cite its source. Prefer current constitutional/enabling material over historical minutes. Never infer powers from the body name. Produce the standard Body Profile plus a short list of unresolved questions.

## Regression test

Provide a renamed committee with old minutes under its predecessor title. PASS only if continuity/history and current authority are kept distinct.
