# Modern Democratic Services

Public-safe reference architecture and laboratory for an excellent modern Democratic Services operating model.

## Status

**DESIGN / RESEARCH FOUNDATION — NOT YET A WEBSITE**

Research position: 23 September 2026.

This directory is intentionally isolated from the repository's existing mixed reusable assets. It contains no employer-specific, confidential, personal or unpublished workplace information.

## Purpose

Develop a practical, evidence-led handbook and toolset that helps a Democratic Services officer:

- locate the controlling authority;
- understand committees, boards, powers and relationships;
- research unfamiliar governance questions;
- improve the quality and readiness of decision papers;
- plan meeting and decision workflows;
- preserve accurate records and institutional memory;
- use ordinary Microsoft 365 and bounded AI tools proportionately;
- improve one recurring irritation at a time, with evidence.

The project treats Democratic Services as **institutional decision infrastructure**, not merely meeting administration.

## Core model

1. **Authority** — law, constitution, delegation and procedure.
2. **Knowledge** — bodies, powers, sources, history and recurring business.
3. **Decision Flow** — proposal to evidence, meeting, decision, action and memory.
4. **Workbench** — Microsoft 365, structured information, templates and bounded AI engines.
5. **Assurance & Improvement** — verify, measure, test and improve.

## Publication boundary

Nothing in this project should depend on obscurity for protection.

Do not add:

- personal data;
- confidential or unpublished employer information;
- private correspondence or screenshots;
- credentials or security-sensitive configuration;
- identifiable commentary about colleagues;
- unverified employer-specific claims;
- substantial copyrighted source text where linking/citation is sufficient.

The eventual public site must remain useful if its author changes employer tomorrow.
