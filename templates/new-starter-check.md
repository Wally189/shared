# New Starter Reconstruction Check

A competent new starter should be able to establish without relying on one colleague's memory:

- what democratic bodies exist;
- what each does;
- its current authority;
- how bodies relate;
- meeting cycles;
- recurring business;
- current outstanding actions;
- canonical record locations;
- main local procedural rules;
- professional ownership of specialist judgements;
- material historic decisions;
- current unknowns or disputed interpretations.

For each failed question, record the missing source/control rather than merely briefing the person verbally.
