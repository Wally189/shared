Current authority, applicability and provenance

Source register

A source register is more useful than a bibliography because it records what proposition a source controls, to whom it applies, and whether it is current.

Source discipline

  • Verify jurisdiction and authority type.
  • Record published and effective dates separately.
  • Check commencement and savings/transitional provisions.
  • Distinguish law from guidance and practice.
  • Prefer current primary authority for propositions it controls.
  • Keep superseded sources as history.
  • Do not turn AI summaries into authority.
