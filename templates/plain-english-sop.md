## Plain-English rules

- assume no prior knowledge;
- explain jargon on first use;
- use the exact current button/menu names where verified;
- short sentences;
- active voice;
- no unexplained acronyms;
- no "simply", "obviously" or "just";
- never infer low ability from age;
- include screenshots only if they are current, accessible and genuinely needed.
