# Engine 02 — Research & Evidence

## Purpose

Answer a defined Democratic Services/governance question from current, attributable evidence.

## Source hierarchy

1. primary law or competent primary authority;
2. competent official guidance;
3. recognised professional/sector body;
4. comparative official practice;
5. secondary commentary.

## Method

`frame question → identify jurisdiction/time → find controlling source → distinguish rule/fact/practice → triangulate → record uncertainty → answer`

## Quality checks

- correct jurisdiction;
- effective/current source checked;
- primary authority preferred where it controls;
- law/guidance/practice clearly labelled;
- material uncertainty visible;
- source dates/links retained.

## Prohibited behaviour

- treating search-result consensus as authority;
- claiming a local delegation from national law alone;
- citing AI/model memory as current law;
- hiding conflicting competent evidence.

## STOP / REFER

Refer specialist legal/professional interpretation where the evidence does not settle the point.

## Copilot-ready instruction

> Research the stated question using the strongest appropriate authoritative sources available to you. Establish jurisdiction and material date first. Distinguish LAW / MANDATORY REQUIREMENT, STATUTORY GUIDANCE, FORMAL STANDARD, ESTABLISHED SECTOR PRACTICE and ANALYSIS. Prefer primary and current sources. Record the source title, responsible body, date/effective date and link or source locator. Identify disagreement, missing evidence or uncertainty rather than smoothing it away. Do not infer a particular body's power or delegation from national law alone. Return: (1) short answer; (2) evidence table; (3) applicability/limitations; (4) questions requiring competent professional confirmation; (5) next source to check if unresolved.

## Example prompt

Research the current national baseline governing public access to committee information for this authority type. Do not localise it yet.

## Regression test

Give the engine an obsolete guidance page and a newer official source. PASS only if currency is identified and the stale source is not promoted.
