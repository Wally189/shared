About this project

A deliberately plain public research project about Democratic Services.

Publication test

Would the material remain acceptable if read tomorrow by a chief executive, Monitoring Officer, councillor, journalist, union representative and the software provider? If not, do not publish it.

Exclude

  • personal data;
  • confidential or unpublished workplace information;
  • private correspondence or internal screenshots;
  • credentials or security-sensitive configuration;
  • interpersonal commentary or identifiable criticism;
  • undocumented employer procedures;
  • substantial copyrighted documents where linking/analysis is sufficient;
  • employer-specific claims not established by public authoritative sources.

Accessibility

This independent site voluntarily targets WCAG 2.2 AA principles: semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, reflow, readable contrast, meaningful links and no essential JavaScript. That is a quality target, not a claim that public-sector accessibility regulations necessarily apply to this independent site.

Maintenance

Law and product behaviour change. Material propositions require currentness checks. Engines, templates and prompts should have versions, review triggers and retirement rules.

Independent and unofficial. This is a reference model, not employer procedure, legal advice or official governance guidance. Verify current law, authority type, current constitution and competent professional advice before relying on a proposition.
