Committees & boards

A committee directory is not enough. The useful object is a source-linked Body Profile that explains what the body is, what it can do, how it relates to others, and when that information was last verified.

Body Profile

AreaMinimum useful fields
IdentityBody ID, current name, current/dormant/abolished, predecessor and successor.
PurposeRole, remit, decision/advisory/scrutiny status.
AuthorityStatutory/constitutional basis, terms of reference, delegated and reserved functions.
CompositionMembership rules, chair, substitutes, quorum and political-balance rules where applicable.
RelationshipsReports to, receives from, scrutinises, appoints/is appointed by, joint interfaces.
OperationMeeting cycle, agenda timetable, publication/public-participation rules, recording/webcasting.
BusinessRecurring items, forward work, significant historic decisions, dependencies.
AdministrationSecretariat role, professional interfaces, canonical sources, last verified date, uncertainty.

Old minutes are valuable history. They are not automatically evidence of current powers.

Public examples

Established practice example West Yorkshire Combined Authority publishes its constitution as navigable parts, including responsibility for functions, terms of reference, access-to-information rules and scrutiny standing orders, and says it reviews the constitution annually.

Transferable improvement example Bristol City Council's Committee Model Review Group shows governance arrangements themselves can be reviewed for effectiveness.
