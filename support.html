Case Bus anatomy

GOAL

The bounded outcome.

SOURCES

Stable evidence identifiers and versions.

FACT / PRACTICE

Verified propositions kept separate from local habit and unverified claims.

AUTHORITY

Legal, constitutional, professional, permission and scope limits.

CAPABILITY

AVAILABLE, LIMITED, NOT AVAILABLE or UNKNOWN.

UNKNOWN / RISK

Contradictions, gaps, failure modes and dependencies remain visible.

HANDOFF

The next engine, human role or workplace tool and its exact bounded question.

VERIFY / STOP

The evidence needed before continuation and the condition that ends the route.

StateReference meaning
AVAILABLEObserved or authoritatively confirmed for the required use.

NOT AVAILABLEConfirmed unavailable or not permitted for the required use.
UNKNOWNNot yet checked. A plan should not depend upon it.

Verification without bureaucracy

The gate is intentionally short. It asks whether the sources are real, the authority chain is correct, the proposed tools actually exist in the required form, information handling is proportionate, commitments have not been invented, the change is reversible, specialist findings agree, and a competent professional role is required.

PASSevidence supports the bounded step
PASS WITH LIMITScontinue only inside stated constraints
STOP / REFERmissing authority, capability or professional judgement blocks continuation
