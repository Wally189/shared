Borrow the discipline, not the costume

Best practice from elsewhere

Democratic Services does not need to pretend to be Parliament, an airline, an NHS trust or a company board. It can still borrow methods that solve the same underlying problem, provided legal and constitutional differences are preserved.

Source sectorProblem it solves / useful borrowingDo not copySafe Democratic Services test
UK Parliament / select committeesEvidence-led inquiry: clear question, calls for evidence, oral/written evidence, recommendations and response tracking.Parliamentary powers, privilege or procedure that local bodies do not possess.Use an evidence matrix for one scrutiny inquiry and test whether recommendations become better traceable.
Central government boards / secretariatsClear board purpose, roles, information quality, challenge and documented governance.Whitehall departmental machinery or ministerial conventions as if they were local law.Apply a short board-paper information-quality check to one existing report.
NHS provider governanceBoard/committee framework, agenda/minute discipline, governance effectiveness and explicit accountability.NHS statutory duties, provider-licence requirements or clinical-governance structures.Compare one public board pack's navigation, accessibility and committee reporting with a local pack.
Higher educationEvidence that governance must be both adequate on paper and effective in practice; periodic effectiveness review.Academic-governance structures or student-regulatory duties outside their context.Ask whether the current committee map is merely documented or demonstrably works.
Courts / tribunalsExplicit directions, owner/date clarity and disciplined handling of non-compliance.Judicial powers, sanctions or adversarial procedure.Improve one internal deadline notice so action, date, consequence and escalation are unambiguous.
Company secretarial practiceBoard-pack readiness, concise minutes, action schedules, corporate records and governance adviser role.Companies Act duties as if they applied to public authorities.Test an action schedule with owner/deadline/status and a materiality rule for what enters minutes.
Government project delivery / riskNamed accountable owner, dependencies, assurance, benefits, risk and continuous-improvement assessment.Heavy project bureaucracy for routine committee administration.Use a one-page owner/dependency/acceptance/rollback card for a single improvement experiment.
Aviation / high-reliability safetyJust Culture: encourage reporting, distinguish honest error from serious misconduct, learn from incidents.Safety-critical regulation, aviation reporting thresholds or punitive models.Run a no-blame review of one administrative near miss and change the process rather than merely reminding staff.
Records managementLifecycle ownership, retrieval, retention, disposal and continuity independent of one person's memory.Universal retention periods without local schedule/legal basis.Choose one recurring record type and prove who owns it, where it lives, how it is found and what governs retention.
Digital service designUnderstand users, solve the whole problem, accessibility, small iterations, measures and reliable operation.Treating Democratic Services as a consumer app or redesigning for novelty.Observe one recurring task end-to-end and remove one source of duplicate entry.
